Step 0Before you start

  • Open the site and press Ctrl + Shift + R once so you see the latest version.
  • Empty the bag first, so you can show adding a product live.
  • Keep a phone (or the browser's phone view) ready for the mobile part.
  • Close other tabs so nothing pops up during the demo.
Check these before the client sees them
  • Prices: the site now shows prices in Naira (₦), but the product prices are still the old small numbers, so a dress shows as ₦180. Either update the real prices before the demo, or tell the client “prices are placeholders for now.”
  • “EU delivery” still appears as a small tag at the top of the Collections page. If the business is Naira-based, ask me to change it.
  • WhatsApp number on the site is +91 63056 15898. Confirm this is the client's real number, or say it is a test number.
  • Sample text: the chat on the Contact page, the photo titles in the Lookbook and a few short lines (values, FAQ answers) are example wording. Present them as “ready for your final text.”

6Cart and how ordering works

The side bag

ShowClick the cart icon. Change a quantity, then close it.

“Clicking the cart opens a small panel from the side. The customer can change quantities or remove items, see the subtotal, and go to checkout, without leaving the page they're on.”

Cart page and checkout

ShowClick “View full bag”, then “Continue to checkout”. Point at the steps at the top and the progress bar while you type an email.

“This is the full cart, and then the checkout. The steps at the top show where they are: Bag, Your details, Confirm on WhatsApp. The customer fills in contact and delivery details, chooses shipping and payment, and the progress bar fills up as they go. The order summary stays visible on the side.”

How the order reaches you

Customer adds pieces and fills in the checkout
They press “Confirm order on WhatsApp”
WhatsApp opens with the full order already written
They press Send. It arrives on your WhatsApp
You confirm, send delivery cost and payment details

“This is the important part. There is no complicated payment system. When the customer confirms, WhatsApp opens with the whole order already written: products, sizes, total, name, address and an order reference. They just press Send, and you receive it on your WhatsApp. Then you confirm personally and send the delivery cost and payment details. It's simple, personal, and there are no monthly fees.”

10Phone view

ShowOpen the site on a phone. Tap the menu icon, scroll the home page, add a product to the bag.

“Most customers will shop on their phone, so every page was designed and tested for phones, from small ones like the iPhone SE to large ones, and tablets too. The menu becomes a simple icon, categories fit on one screen, and on product and cart pages the price and main button stay fixed at the bottom so it's always easy to buy.”

11Questions the client may ask

“Can customers pay online by card?”
“Right now orders are confirmed on WhatsApp and you send payment details, which keeps things personal and has no fees. Card payment can be added later if you want it.”
“Can I add or change products myself?”
“Products are stored in simple files with name, price, sizes, colours and photos. We can go through the easiest way for you to manage them after this demo.” (Check with the developer before promising an admin panel.)
“Does it work on phones?”
“Yes. Every page was tested on small phones, normal phones, tablets, laptops and large screens.”
“Is it fast?”
“Yes. Photos are optimised to load quickly, and the site is built as fast static pages, so it's also cheap to host.”
“Can we change the text and photos?”
“Yes. Headlines, taglines, photos and prices can all be changed. Some text on the site today is example wording ready for your final version.”
“Where do the WhatsApp messages go?”
“To the WhatsApp number set on the site. We just need to confirm it's your correct business number.”

Closing

“So that's the full journey: a customer arrives, is inspired by the brand, finds a piece easily, adds it to the bag and sends the order to you on WhatsApp in a few taps. It looks luxury, works on every device, and stays personal, just like your brand. I'd love to hear your thoughts, and anything you'd like to change.”

Tip: speak slowly, let each animation play for a second, and pause after each section to ask “Any questions so far?”
